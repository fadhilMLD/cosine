Pricing strategy

Pricing strategy guide for subscription businesses

Pricing strategy is not just about choosing a number. It is about building a disciplined revenue architecture that can support conversion, retention, and sustainable unit economics across plans and discounts.

Keywords: pricing strategy guide, subscription pricing, discount policy, revenue architecture, annual plans, conversion rates, pricing discipline

Review the plan architecture first

Before changing prices, check the structure of your plans. Free, monthly, annual, and enterprise tiers all influence how customers convert and how revenue is recognized. If the plan architecture is unclear, the pricing discussion becomes guesswork.

Checklist for subscription pricing

  • Define the value gap between each tier.
  • Set a clear discount policy and keep it consistent.
  • Review whether annual plans improve cash flow or just reduce margin.
  • Model conversion rates by segment instead of using one blended rate.
  • Check that pricing still supports the target gross margin.

Why discount policy matters

Discounts can help acquisition, but they also train buyers to wait for a better price. A pricing strategy should say when a discount is allowed, how deep it can go, and what it is meant to achieve.

What to report back

Report the plan architecture, the price sensitivity risks, and the expected change in revenue mix if the new pricing ships. That is enough to make the next discussion concrete.
